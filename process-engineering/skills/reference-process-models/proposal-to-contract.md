# Reference model: proposal to contract

1. **Opportunity intake.** Where opportunities come from (RFP portals, referrals, existing clients); who logs them; the record of each opportunity.
2. **Bid decision.** Go or no-go criteria; who decides; capacity and conflict checks.
3. **Requirements.** Reading the RFP or brief; compliance matrix; questions to the issuer and their deadline; amendments.
4. **Team and plan.** Proposal manager; writers and reviewers by section; the schedule back from the due date; kickoff.
5. **Content.** Reuse of past content (library, boilerplate); resumes and past performance; pricing and its assumptions; approvals of pricing.
6. **Review.** Review stages (draft, red team, final); who signs off; legal and contract review of terms.
7. **Production and submission.** Formatting and compliance check; submission method and confirmation of receipt; the deadline's time zone.
8. **After submission.** Clarifications and orals; best and final offers; debrief on a loss; the win or loss recorded with the reason.
9. **Award and contract.** Negotiation; contract review and signature authority; hand-off to delivery (scope, budget, people); kickoff with the client.
10. **Learning and records.** Win rate and pipeline reporting; content library updated from the submission; where every version and the submitted copy live.

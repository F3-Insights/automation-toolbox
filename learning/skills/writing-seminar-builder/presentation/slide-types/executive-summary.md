## When to reach for it

Reach for this when a senior audience needs the entire argument on a single page, stated answer-first, and the slide has to stand alone if it is forwarded without you in the room. It is the McKinsey-style executive summary: one governing thought at the top that is the recommendation itself, then three to five mutually exclusive supporting points that each summarize a section of the deck, optionally anchored by one small figure that makes the headline claim concrete. Use it as the opening orient slide of a recommendation deck (the answer the rest of the deck earns) or as the closing consolidation right before next-steps. Pick executive-summary over a recap or send-off when the job is to carry the full logic in writing for a reader who skims titles; pick recap when you only need three section takeaways, and send-off when you want one substantive line to land out loud. Keep the supports MECE and bold-lead so the structure reads in one pass, and frame the governing thought as a forward recommendation, never as a correction of what the audience does today (F3 voice: positive and additive). One gold accent only: it rides the governing thought rule and the single figure.

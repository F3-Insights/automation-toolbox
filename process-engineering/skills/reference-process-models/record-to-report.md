# Reference model: record to report

1. **Transactions.** Every source of entries (billing, payables, payroll, bank, card, inventory) and when each is complete for the period; cut-off rules.
2. **Recurring and period-end entries.** Accruals, prepaids, depreciation, allocations, revenue recognition; who prepares and who reviews each.
3. **Reconciliations.** Bank and cash; receivables and payables subledgers to the ledger; intercompany; fixed assets; payroll liabilities; inventory; suspense accounts.
4. **Review.** Flux and variance analysis against budget and prior period; review of journal entries above a threshold; sign-off.
5. **Close calendar.** The checklist with owners and due days; status tracking; what holds the close open.
6. **Reporting.** Statements produced; management reporting package; board and lender reporting; covenants.
7. **Adjustments after close.** Late entries; reopening a period; audit adjustments.
8. **Controls and records.** Segregation of duties; journal approval; access to the ledger; supporting documents kept with each entry; the audit trail.

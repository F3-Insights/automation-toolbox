# Reference model: procure to pay

1. **Need.** How a need is raised (requisition, reorder point, project); who may raise one.
2. **Approval.** Spend authority by amount and category; budget check; emergency purchases.
3. **Vendor.** Vendor selection and onboarding (tax forms, banking details, insurance); approved-vendor list; changes to a vendor's bank details and how they are verified.
4. **Sourcing.** Quotes or bids required above a threshold; contract or catalogue pricing.
5. **Purchase order.** PO creation and approval; PO sent to the vendor; vendor acknowledgement; PO changes and cancellations.
6. **Receipt.** Goods or service receipt recorded; inspection; partial receipts; rejected goods and returns to vendor; damage claims against the carrier.
7. **Invoice.** Invoice intake (channel, format); matching to PO and receipt (two- or three-way); price and quantity variances and who resolves them; credit notes.
8. **Payment.** Payment run frequency; approval of the run; payment methods; early-payment discounts; vendor statements and reconciliation.
9. **Accruals.** Goods received not invoiced; services delivered but not billed at period end.
10. **Records and controls.** Duplicate-invoice checks; segregation of duties (requisition, approval, receipt, payment); audit trail; 1099 or equivalent reporting.

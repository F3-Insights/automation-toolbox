# Meridian Manufacturing: Company Overview

*Last revised: 2026-04. Owner: CEO.*

## What we do

Meridian Manufacturing designs, builds, and installs custom industrial machinery for two end-markets:

1. **Food processing**: primarily proteins (poultry, pork). Cutting, portioning, packaging lines.
2. **Beverage bottling**: small- and mid-batch craft and specialty beverages. Filling, capping, labeling lines.

We are not a catalog manufacturer. Every line we ship is engineered to the customer's plant, product mix, and throughput targets. The closest comparison is a custom systems integrator that happens to also own the manufacturing.

## Scale and structure

- **Revenue:** $120M (FY2025, full year)
- **EBITDA:** $14M (~12%)
- **Headcount:** 380 FTE
- **Facility:** Single 220,000 sqft owned facility in Akron, OH (manufacturing + engineering + corporate)
- **Ownership:** Closely held. Founder + CEO holds 64%. Three minority partners (operations, engineering, finance) hold ~10% each.
- **Capital posture:** Conservative. ~$9M cash on hand, $5M unused LOC, no term debt.

## Customer base

- **~85 active accounts** across the two end-markets.
- **Concentration:** Top 10 customers = 62% of revenue. Top 1 = 14%. Top 3 = 31%.
- **Geography (revenue mix):** 41% Midwest, 18% Southeast, 22% Southwest (TX/AZ/NM/OK), 19% other.
- **Sales cycle:** Average 7 months from first conversation to signed contract. Long-tail customers can take 18 months.
- **Average contract value:** ~$850k. Range: $250k–$3.4M.
- **Repeat rate:** ~55% of any year's revenue comes from prior customers (expansions, line replacements, new plants).

## What makes us different

The competitive set is mostly catalog-based systems integrators in our segment. Their advantage is speed-to-quote and price. Ours is:

1. **In-house engineering.** We do not subcontract the design layer. 38 engineers, ~30 with food/beverage domain experience.
2. **Install responsibility.** We commission the equipment on the customer's floor. Most competitors hand off to a third party at delivery.
3. **Repeat customers, not transactions.** ~55% repeat rate; some customers have bought 6+ lines from us across 15 years.

## Strategic context (current)

- Revenue grew 11% in FY2024, 14% in FY2025. Pipeline supports ~12% growth in FY2026 if we deliver on commitments.
- **31% of recent growth came from SW customers (TX/AZ/NM)**: significantly above their share of installed base. This is the load-bearing trend behind the Texas-expansion question now in front of leadership.
- The facility in Akron is at ~88% capacity utilization on a 2-shift basis. Adding a third shift is possible but degrades engineering-to-floor turnaround and was rejected as a sustained option in 2024.
- The current strategic question (Q2 2026): **Whether to open a second production facility in San Antonio, TX, to serve regional customers.** That decision is the subject of the current pre-mortem.

## Cultural identity (in 3 lines)

- Engineers run the company. Marketing and sales report up through engineering leadership, not the other way around.
- We say no to customers more often than we say yes. We don't take work outside our two end-markets.
- Decisions get made in writing. Conversations confirm; documents commit.

# Session log

One file per delivered session (seminar, lab, workshop, talk), written the same day while the room is still in memory. The post-delivery counterpart of `meeting-summary`; it feeds the next session's design and the next deck's content map.

```
# [Topic, short title] · YYYY-MM-DD · [venue or client]

## Audience
Who was in the room: roles, count, what they already knew, what they came for.

## Topic, one line
The spine sentence from the content map, as delivered.

## Outline, what was covered
The beats as they actually ran, with times if recorded. Note where the plan and the
delivery diverged and why.

## Tips, snippets and details worth keeping
Anything said or shown that landed and is not yet in a deck or a reference file.
Verbatim where possible.

## Teaching-style notes, session-specific
What the presenter did that worked or did not, for the presenter's own record.

## Participant feedback and quotes worth harvesting
Verbatim, attributed only if the person would expect it. Questions asked are feedback.

## What worked
Three bullets.

## What did not
Three bullets, each with what to change next time.

## Tags
Comma-separated: topic, audience type, format, series.
```

Rules: write it before reading the recording, then correct it from the recording. A quote that cannot be found in the transcript is removed, not softened.

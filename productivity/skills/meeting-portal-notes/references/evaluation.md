# Evaluation and stopping rule

A passing case captures all material accepted actions and relevant notes, with no unsupported actor, scope, deadline, decision or current-state claim. A source reviewer should be able to verify each entry. Ambiguity can remain explicit; inventing certainty to fill a field is a failure.

Review independently against raw source:
1. Enumerate accepted actions before looking at the draft. Compare omissions and additions; record each miss and its source turns, not just a score.
2. Check actors, deliverables, conditions, acceptance and timing. An accurate quote attached to a stronger claim still fails. Proposals must survive as proposals.
3. Check the summary and supporting notes for material omissions, numerical errors, lost negations/corrections, and hypotheses presented as facts.
4. Verify identities/project associations from Portal evidence. Check existing task matches and whether apparently supporting records derive from this same meeting. Keep meeting-time evidence distinct from later state.
5. Read the result as the participant: is it useful and clear, without repeated background, spurious questions, or a wall of process commentary?

Revise demonstrated errors, rerun evidence checks, then review changed claims. Stop a case when no material errors remain in the source review; do not iterate merely to change wording. Preserve residual ambiguity. If a material error keeps recurring, report it and retain the case as failing rather than quietly narrowing its expected answer.

Keep frozen cases and per-version results. Include meetings of different types and held-out cases with corrections, silent delegation, ambiguous actors, stale context and existing tasks. Never feed gold answers into the generating pass. Human adjudication resolves disputed labels; model reviewers are useful but not an accuracy oracle. Two successful meetings are evidence of improvement, not a validated production reliability rate. Track API/tool calls and elapsed effort; local-agent work is not free simply because there is no separate provider bill.

A case with a material context gap can pass extraction while remaining pending for catalog placement/execution. Ask a targeted clarification, retain the original uncertainty, and rerun the affected checks after the user answers. Do not claim the catalog is ready if the answer could change its owner, scope or destination.

# Hands-on lab kit (60 minutes)

The four files that make a one-hour hands-on training session run, for a room that has to leave with something working, not something understood. The lab is the fourth deck purpose in `writing-seminar-builder`, alongside teaching, consulting and financial.

## 1. Run of show

- **Prep checklist**, completed the day before: accounts provisioned, connectors authorised on a test account, the demo run end to end once, the handout printed or linked, the hold slide up.
- **Blocks**, each `H:MM to H:MM · Title`, with the only lecture block named as such:
  - 0:00 to 0:05 **Welcome: what you'll leave with.** Three outcomes, stated as things the participant will have done.
  - 0:05 to 0:15 **Framing.** The one lecture block. The mental model, no more.
  - 0:15 to 0:25 **Checkpoint 1: logged in and connected.** "You're done when" gate: the connector shows green.
  - 0:25 to 0:40 **Checkpoint 2: build and run your first workflow.** Gate: it produced output from their own data.
  - 0:40 to 0:50 **Checkpoint 3: read then draft.** Gate: a draft exists in their system and nothing was sent.
  - 0:50 to 0:57 **Write-path demo, participant drives.** The facilitator narrates; a participant clicks. This week's habit named.
  - 0:57 to 1:00 **Close and parking lot.**
- **Straggler protocol.** Who helps whom, when the room moves on without a straggler, what the straggler does after. Written down so the facilitator does not decide live.

Every checkpoint has a **"you're done when"** line. A participant who cannot say yes to it is not done, whatever they feel.

## 2. Participant handout

One page. The three checkpoints with their done-conditions, the exact clicks for each, the habit to try this week, and where to ask for help. No framing content; that was the lecture.

## 3. Prework note

Sent by the sponsor a week ahead. Three bullets: what to have ready (accounts, a laptop, one real task from their week), what will happen, what they will leave with. Under 120 words.

## 4. Deck rules for a lab

- **Hold slide** up while people arrive and during every checkpoint: the checkpoint number, the done-condition, the help channel.
- **Zero builds.** No animations; the room is looking at their own screen, not yours.
- **No offer slide.** A lab is delivery, not a pitch.
- Slides exist only for the framing block and the checkpoint gates.

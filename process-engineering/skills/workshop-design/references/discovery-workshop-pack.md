# Discovery workshop pack

Four documents produced for a paid leadership discovery workshop, in the order they are written. Used by `workshop-design` and the `workshop-orchestrator`; process visuals for the room come from the `process-flow-workstream` method.

## 1. `collected-ideas.csv`

One row per idea, complaint, or request heard before the workshop. Built during sponsor prep; the workshop presents it back grouped.

```
"Source","Evidence","Group","Category","Item","Notes"
```

- **Source**: who said it (a name; this file is internal).
- **Evidence**: where and when, e.g. "starter list, week 1; sponsor call, week 3".
- **Group**: the cluster it belongs to once clustered (blank until Phase 2 of the brief).
- **Category**: a small integer code for the theme; define the codes in the brief.
- **Item**: the idea in the speaker's words, one line.
- **Notes**: anything that changes how it lands in the room.

## 2. INTERNAL prep brief

Marked "INTERNAL, consultant's eyes only, do not circulate" on line 2. Six sections:

1. **Who is in the room.** Roster with role, what each person told you, and what they want out of the day.
2. **What you actually heard.** How the inputs (starter list, 1:1 calls, documents) converge into three to five clusters. Name the clusters; these become the workshop's spine.
3. **Landmines, keep these OUT of the room.** Each one with the room-safe version of the same point. This section exists so the consultant never improvises a sensitive framing live.
4. **Open items before the day.** Data not yet received, people not yet confirmed, logistics.
5. **Questions to have in your pocket.** For a silent room or a derailed one.
6. **Your commercial posture.** What the workshop is priced at, what it leads to, what not to promise.

## 3. Facilitation plan

- **The one-sentence goal.** What the room agrees by the end.
- **Approach.** Two or three sentences on how the day is shaped and why.
- **Room mechanics.** Seating, materials, who captures, the parking lot.
- **Run of show.** `HH:MM to HH:MM · Title (N min)` per block, each with: what happens, who speaks, the artifact on screen, the decision or output. A typical shape: sponsor opens (10) · what I heard, the inputs grouped into one picture (25) · as-is, where the work and time go (25) · break · clusters and options (30) · impact/effort placement, cap three (30) · owners, first artifacts, dates (15) · close and next steps (10).
- **Dependencies as facts.** What has to be true for the next step to start, stated without judgment.

## 4. Post-workshop summary

Issued within three business days. Three to four pages. Headings:

1. **Purpose and participants.**
2. **What we heard.** One sub-heading per cluster, plus a cross-cutting sub-heading. Paraphrased, unattributed.
3. **What the room prioritised.** The three items, each with owner, first artifact, date.
4. **Parking lot.** Everything not chosen, kept visible.
5. **Recommended next steps.** What the consultant proposes, framed as a recommendation.
6. **Scope boundary.** Stated once, plainly: what this engagement will and will not do.
